Common Questions

Answers before you need to ask

How do you ensure our designs remain confidential from political opponents?

Every file moves through a restricted, access-controlled workflow — only your assigned production team sees the designs, and nothing is shared, previewed, or stored outside that secure channel until you approve release.

What is your typical turnaround time for a high-volume rush order?

Most rush orders move through our partner network in 24–48 hours, even at high volume. Flag the rush toggle on your campaign brief and we'll confirm a realistic delivery window before we start production.

Do your packages cover both Nairobi and Nyeri distribution?

Yes — Nairobi and Nyeri are both core service regions, with the same delivery guarantees in either location. Other counties can be arranged on request; just note it in your campaign brief.

What happens if we need to change messaging mid-campaign?

Ground materials get fast reprint priority, and digital assets are templated so updates go out the same day. Tell us the change through your usual contact channel and we'll adjust the production queue.
